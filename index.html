About the business

We started with one second-hand drum roaster and a simple belief: good coffee should be fresh, fairly bought and easy to get.

Today we supply households, cafes, offices and event organisers with single-origin and blended beans, run barista training, and offer custom-branded coffee for businesses. Every batch is roasted to order, tasted by our team, and packed the same week it is sent.

Our mission: to make fresh, ethically sourced coffee part of everyday life.
